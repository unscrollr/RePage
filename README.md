"The internet was designed to be read in pages. Infinite scroll was invented to steal your time. We're taking it back."

Scrolling is addictive. It's a form of Dark Pattern exploitation - infinite scroll is worse, but not many people know that scrolling itself is the root of the problem. It works by mimicking the visual flow that usually motivates us to move around our environment. Engagement increases from between 15-50% when UI switches from pagination to scrolling . This function disproportionately impacts neurodivergent people and children.
